# Seeds of Genius: Player Guide

*Seeds of Genius: George Washington Carver and the Power of Science* is a
cozy story game for ages 8 to 13. You explore a small town, help George
Washington Carver with seven science quests, and finish by planning a
project of your own. It runs in a web browser, needs no account, and saves
by itself.

**Play it:** open `/games/seeds-of-genius/index.html` on the Learning
Adventures site. A laptop or desktop works best; tablets and phones work too.

## Getting started

1. Press **Start a new game** and choose how you look (boy or girl, skin tone, hair,
   outfit, an accessory). You can change it later.
2. The **Next** box in the top-left corner always says what to do.
3. Walk to **Carver** (the man in the suit with a flower on his lapel, next
   to the greenhouse). An arrow points the way.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Mouse | Touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Walk | W A S D or arrow keys (Shift to hurry) | Click the ground | Arrow pad, or tap the ground |
| Talk, open, use | E, Space or Enter | Click a person, sign or door | "Use" button |
| Choose an answer | 1 to 4, or arrow keys and Enter | Click | Tap |
| Journal / Bag | J / I | Toolbar buttons | Toolbar buttons |
| Sound on or off | M | Toolbar button | Toolbar button |
| Settings | Esc | Toolbar button | Toolbar button |

## How a chapter works

1. **Carver gives you a quest** and tells you why it matters.
2. **Talk to the helpers** he names. They give you items or clues (a
   notebook, a map, a kit...). A **!** above someone means they have
   something for you.
3. **Use the items** in the chapter's activity: look closely, sort, plan,
   invent, test.
4. **Go back to Carver** and tell him what you found. He asks you a
   question, shows you a memory from his real life, and gives you a
   keepsake card.

Mistakes are fine. Wrong answers explain why, and hints get more helpful
each time you try again. Nothing is ever lost, and you can replay any
activity.

## The chapters

| # | Chapter | You will... |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Seeds for the Windowsill | fetch a seed packet and learn how quests work |
| 1 | A Seed Is Planted | look closely at a garden and tell observations from guesses |
| 2 | Science Against the Odds | follow Carver's path through school |
| 3 | The Soil Speaks | test planting plans to help tired soil |
| 4 | The Peanut Isn't Just a Peanut | invent, test and improve a snack |
| 5 | Science for the People | help two farmers with practical, free ideas |
| 6 | A Scientist's Method | run a fair experiment in Carver's greenhouse |
| 7 | Your Turn to Plant the Seeds | plan your own community project |

When you finish, **Your journey** shows everything you did. You can see it
again on the shelf in your cottage.

## Your journal

Press **J**. The journal has:

- **Quest:** Carver's current quest, who to talk to, and your items.
- **Bag:** everything you collected. Look closer at any item.
- **Map:** the town and your chapter progress.
- **Talks:** replay any conversation.
- **About:** sources, credits and privacy.
- **For grown-ups:** what each chapter teaches, talking points, and
  printable activities.

## Saving

The game saves by itself after every important moment. In **Settings
(Esc)** you can also:

- **Save now.**
- **Download a save file**, to move your game to another computer (load it
  there from the same place).
- **Start over** (the game asks you to confirm first).

Saves stay in your browser. Clearing the browser's site data erases them, so
download a save file first if you need one.

## Settings that can help

Larger text, slower or faster dialogue, reduced motion, sound and music
volume, and on-screen touch controls are all in **Settings (Esc)**. Parts
of Chapter 2 talk about racism; every one of them can be skipped and read
later.

## Stuck?

- Read the **Next** box, or follow the arrow.
- Talk to **Carver**: he always gives a nudge.
- Open the **journal (J)** to see who you still need to talk to.
- If the screen looks wrong, reload the page and press **Continue**. Your
  progress is kept.
